We were buying power supplies directly from a manufacturer in China in qtys of 100.  You can find ones with the same specs on Amazon.  NOTE that the barrel connector ID is 2.5mm, NOT 2.1mm.  Both versions exist, so be sure to check the description.  We used a 6A 72W power supply in order to get the torque out of the motor we liked for tight skeins.  I would NOT recommend a bigger power supply as it could allow the motor to pull so much power that it could potentially break your fingers if something goes wrong and your hand gets stuck in the skein.  A lower power 5A 60W power supply will also work, but will not twist skeins as tight and might cut out early, before the full time has elapsed.

We purchased the removable AC power cord separately as we sold to other countries with different wall plugs.  See the SkeinTwister BOM for purchasing information.
